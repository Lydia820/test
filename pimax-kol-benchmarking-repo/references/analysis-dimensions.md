# Multi-dimensional diagnosis

Use this map to avoid reducing success to “good content” or “good conversion.” Cover dimensions supported by evidence and explicitly mark unknowns.

| Dimension | Questions | Useful evidence | Replication decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business outcome | Did orders, subtotal/day, AOV, new-customer share, high-value mix, or repeat sales change? | Approved orders and price book | What outcome should be copied? |
| Audience and market | Was the audience aligned by use case, language, country, hardware level, or buyer maturity? | Geography, comments, audience backend, customer country | Which Pimax product and market fit this partner? |
| Exposure demand | Did the content enter existing demand through game, problem, comparison, news, or non-brand search? | Traffic sources, impressions, query data, public topics | Which demand pool should the next content target? |
| Content proposition | What promise, problem, novelty, proof, or decision question made the content useful? | Title/thumbnail/H1, hook, structure, transcript/article | Which content mechanism is transferable? |
| Proof quality | Did the creator show settings, FPS/frame time, image clarity, comfort, limitations, methodology, or repeated tests? | Video/article evidence and test conditions | What must be demonstrated, not merely claimed? |
| Distribution | Was performance helped by search, browse, suggested video, community, email, social repost, backlinks, or internal links? | Backend source data, public cross-links, referring pages | Which distribution step must Pimax or the partner execute? |
| Cadence and portfolio | Was there one isolated post or a sequence of problem, review, settings, comparison, update, and follow-up content? | Content timeline and interlinking | What sequence should replace a one-off review? |
| Conversion path | Were product links, code, landing page, CTA, comparison, price, and buying route easy to find? | Description, page CTA, link/code orders | Which friction should be removed? |
| Trust and service | Did independence, disclosed drawbacks, troubleshooting, comments, Discord, presales, or after-sales reduce risk? | Comments, guides, community, support references | Which trust asset can be provided without duplicating Pimax support? |
| Commercial context | Were price changes, bundles, deposits, promotions, stock, or launches responsible for part of the lift? | Price/event timeline and order values | Is the mechanism durable outside the offer window? |
| Attribution and measurement | Is the result deterministic, platform-attributed, assisted, inferred, or unknown? | Code, link, UTM, platform, survey | What tracking must be installed before replication? |
| Operational relationship | Did access, samples, early information, assets, review speed, or creator autonomy improve execution? | BD records and creator feedback | What should Pimax change in the collaboration process? |

## Driver map

For each meaningful stage change, build a driver map with four groups:

1. **Creator-controlled:** topic, format, proof, title/thumbnail, article structure, cadence, community, CTA.
2. **Pimax-controlled:** product access, data/assets, landing page, price/promo, tracking, amplification, response speed.
3. **Market-controlled:** competitor launch, game update, expo, seasonality, category demand.
4. **Measurement-controlled:** attribution window, tracking changes, missing backend data, duplicate credit.

Only creator-controlled and Pimax-controlled mechanisms should become direct replication tasks. Market effects become timing guidance; measurement gaps become instrumentation tasks.

## Difference analysis

When comparing a growth-stage item with an earlier baseline item, use the same unit and record actual differences:

- topic/intent;
- title or headline promise;
- thumbnail or search snippet;
- first 30–60 seconds or opening section;
- proof and test design;
- competitor/problem/non-brand coverage;
- CTA, link, code, landing path;
- internal links, follow-ups, community distribution;
- public engagement normalized by age where possible;
- backend reach, CTR, retention, query/page data when available.

Do not assume a difference caused the lift. Identify which difference is measurable, which has a credible mechanism, and which should be tested next.
