# Channel playbooks

## YouTube

### Exposure diagnosis

Establish a channel-relative baseline using recent comparable long-form videos. Compare views at matched age, not current lifetime views. Prefer median to mean when one viral outlier dominates.

Inspect:

- demand source: game/problem/competitor/news versus Pimax-only topic;
- title and thumbnail promise;
- first 30–60 seconds and the moment evidence appears;
- browse, suggested, search, external, subscriber, and returning-viewer mix when backend data exists;
- normalized likes/comments, comment intent, creator replies, pinned comments, and follow-up questions;
- links to tutorials, tools, landing pages, Discord/community, and follow-up videos;
- whether one video seeded a sequence rather than ending as a one-off review.

Public views, likes, and comments cannot reveal impressions, CTR, retention, or traffic-source changes. Mark those as unknown unless screenshots or exports are supplied.

### Conversion diagnosis

Trace the path from problem/interest to proof to decision to purchase. Check whether the creator shows test conditions, measurable results, drawbacks, who the product is for, setup effort, price context, CTA timing, description/pinned links, discount code, dedicated page, and comment/community follow-up.

### Replication brief

Specify:

- target viewer problem and product fit;
- title-angle options and thumbnail concept, without scripting the creator word for word;
- mandatory test scene, hardware/software conditions, evidence, and limitations;
- natural Pimax integration point;
- link/code/landing page and disclosure;
- one follow-up tutorial/comparison and one community distribution action;
- 7-day and 30-day metrics: matched-age views, impressions/CTR if available, retention, engagement quality, clicks, orders, subtotal, and product mix.

## Blog

### Exposure diagnosis

Separate page-level search discovery from site-level strength. Inspect:

- brand versus non-brand intent;
- category, game, problem, settings, comparison, upgrade, review, wait-or-buy, install, and troubleshooting topics;
- title/H1/URL/opening alignment, headings, tables, images, schema, author signals, and last-modified date;
- topic clusters, center pages, long-tail pages, breadcrumbs, internal-link direction, anchor text, orphan pages, and update cadence;
- backlinks, referring pages, newsletter/social distribution, and indexability;
- GSC query/page/country/device trends when available.

Third-party keyword or traffic estimates are discovery aids only. Without GSC, say “public structure suggests a search-entry strategy,” not “traffic increased by X.”

### Conversion diagnosis

Check whether the article answers a decision, shows firsthand evidence, compares alternatives, states drawbacks and fit, links to settings/troubleshooting, explains price/version/bundle context, and provides a visible product path, code, and CTA.

### Replication brief

Specify:

- one primary intent and keyword candidate plus 2–4 close long-tail variants;
- article role: center, decision, long-tail, update, or support page;
- proposed title/H1, unique evidence, section outline, comparison table, FAQ, and schema where relevant;
- required internal links and anchor text;
- product CTA/link/code and disclosure;
- update at 30 and 90 days;
- GSC page/query impressions, clicks, CTR, position; landing sessions, product clicks, orders, subtotal, and assisted evidence.

Tags organize the site and do not replace keyword targeting. Avoid thin tag archives with only one article.

## Same-channel comparison rule

Compare partners on mechanisms and normalized outcomes, not raw scale alone. Use channel-relative baselines:

- YouTube: matched-age views versus channel median, engagement quality, backend reach/CTR/retention where available, clicks and sales.
- Blog: page/query visibility versus the site's own comparable content, internal-link coverage, update behavior, clicks and sales.

If one partner lacks backend data, keep the comparison column `unknown`; do not score missing data as zero.
