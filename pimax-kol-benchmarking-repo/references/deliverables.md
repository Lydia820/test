# Deliverables

## Management brief

Keep the first view concise enough to present verbally:

1. **Decision:** what Pimax should copy, test, or stop.
2. **Outcome change:** stage and monthly movement in orders, subtotal, AOV, and product mix.
3. **Three driver mechanisms:** one line each, separating exposure, conversion, and Pimax enablement.
4. **Evidence confidence:** strongest proof, largest gap, and main confounder.
5. **Next 30 days:** partner, deliverable, owner, deadline, metric, and decision date.

Move detailed tables, keyword lists, and methodology below the brief.

## Benchmark case section

Use this order for each benchmark partner:

- stage comparison;
- linked content examples aligned to stages;
- action differences versus the earlier baseline;
- multi-dimensional driver map;
- exposure mechanisms;
- conversion mechanisms;
- alternative explanations and confidence;
- what same-channel partners can copy;
- what Pimax must provide.

Every linked example should include date, title, URL, stage, observable change, why it matters, and the exact feature another partner should learn from.

## Partner action card

Create one card per KOL/BD owner:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Partner and owner | Name, channel, BD owner |
| Current gap | One benchmark-relative gap supported by evidence |
| Benchmark proof | Specific example/link and mechanism |
| Next deliverable | Exact video/article/follow-up task |
| Creative freedom | What the creator decides |
| Required proof | Test, screenshots, comparison, limitations, or firsthand evidence |
| Exposure plan | Search/recommendation demand, title/keyword, distribution, sequence |
| Conversion path | CTA, product page, code/link, disclosure, support content |
| Pimax enablement | Sample, engineer answer, assets, data, landing page, amplification |
| Measurement | 7/30 days for YouTube; 30/90 days for Blog |
| Decision rule | Scale, iterate, or stop condition |

For implementation requests, the card is incomplete without:

- the first message or meeting opener the business owner can use;
- questions to confirm before product shipment or contract finalization;
- a division of responsibilities between Pimax, the business owner, and the partner;
- a dated or relative timeline from pre-production through the final review;
- the exact backend screenshots or exports required at each checkpoint;
- the comparison evidence behind each action and its intended effect.

Read [activation-planning.md](activation-planning.md) and produce at least one fully specified activation plan. A second plan may be conditional on the first plan's results.

Do not give all partners the same action card. Adapt the mechanism to the partner's audience, format, historical winners, product fit, and operational capacity.

## Five-to-ten partner replication matrix

Build the matrix only after individual partner analysis:

| Partner | Channel | Audience/product fit | Relative exposure baseline | Proven strength | Benchmark gap | Recommended mechanism | Next deliverable | Pimax input | Metric/window | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then prioritize partners into:

- **Replicate now:** strong fit, sufficient evidence, clear transferable gap.
- **Instrument first:** promising fit but missing backend/tracking.
- **Differentiate:** capable partner whose audience or format requires another mechanism.
- **Do not copy yet:** weak fit, insufficient capacity, or confounded performance.

## Brief quality check

Before delivery, confirm:

- The report does not confuse correlation with causation.
- Stage totals are normalized for window length.
- YouTube and Blog have separate logic and metrics.
- Exposure is not reduced to views or keyword lists alone.
- Conversion and service work are distinguished from exposure creation.
- Recommendations include exact examples, links, deliverables, owners, evidence, and review windows.
- Recommendations explain both the benchmark rationale and intended observable effect.
- A business owner could start the partner conversation without inventing missing steps.
- Unknown backend metrics remain unknown.
- The main document is presentation-ready; supporting detail is available without dominating the brief.
